05 / About Akimo

Independent thinking.
Grounded in research.

Akimo Energy Analytics is an independent research and analytical initiative connecting clean-energy scholarship with real-world implementation questions.

Mission

Produce rigorous, accessible, and context-specific analysis that improves understanding of clean-energy opportunities, constraints, and transition pathways.

Regional orientation

Place Costa Rica and Latin America at the centre of the work, drawing on international evidence where comparative analysis can improve local understanding.

Long-term direction

Strengthen connections between quantitative research, public communication, partnerships, and decision-support resources for energy and climate action.

Collaboration

Research, analysis & clean-energy dialogue.
